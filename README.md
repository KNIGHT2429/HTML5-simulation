# HTML5-simulation

1. double-pendulum.html = it starts swinging on its own. Drag the angle sliders and watch the trails change; the "drift ΔE/E₀" number staying tiny is the proof energy is conserved.
2. galton-board.html = balls start dropping automatically. Let it run a minute and the red bell curve settles over the bins.
3. sph-drum.html = the water starts at rest. Slide "drum rotation ω" up slowly; past about 3 rad/s the water climbs the wall and forms a spinning ring. Yanking the slider to the opposite sign is the most fun moment.
